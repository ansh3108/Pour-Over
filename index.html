The Perfect Pawr-Over😸

Mastering the pour-over takes a bit of patience, but the resulting cup is unmatched in flavor. Let's get brewing!

The Purr-fect Setup

Burr Grinder
Gooseneck Kettle
Digital Scale
Paper Filters
Fresh Beans
Dripper

1. The Prep

Heat your water to **205°F**. Place the filter in the dripper and rinse it thoroughly with hot water to remove any papery taste and preheat your vessel. Dump the rinse water. Grind 20g of coffee to a medium-fine consistency and add it to the filter, giving it a gentle shake to level the bed.

2. The Bloom

Start your timer and pour 40g of water, ensuring all the grounds are saturated. Wait for 45 seconds. You should see the coffee bed bubble and expand, this is carbon dioxide escaping, allowing for better extraction later on.

3. The Brew

Pour in slow, concentric circles starting from the center and moving outward. Pour in phases: up to 150g, let it drain slightly, then up to 250g, and finally up to your total weight of 320g. The entire process should take about 3 minutes. Swirl the finished brew and enjoy.
